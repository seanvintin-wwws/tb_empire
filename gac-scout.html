GAC Scout

A one-click bookmark that scouts your Grand Arena opponent from their swgoh.gg GAC history:

  • Their defences: how often each held, and what beat it
  • Their attacks: win rate, and which defences they lose to (set those)
  • When they attack: first and last attack times each round, in your time zone
  • Your relic levels shown next to every counter

1. Install (once)

Computer: show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B), then drag this button onto it:

GAC Scout

Can't drag? Copy everything in the box below, then right-click the bookmarks bar, choose Add page…, name it GAC Scout and paste into the URL field.

2. Use it

  1. Find your opponent on swgoh.gg (any of their pages: profile, GAC, roster).
  2. Click the GAC Scout bookmark.
  3. The first time, enter your own ally code. It's saved in your browser for next time.
  4. Wait about 30 seconds (the tab title counts the rounds). The page turns into the report.

Notes

  • It reads the same public pages you can see on swgoh.gg; nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • By default it uses their recent seasons in the same format (3v3 or 5v5) as their latest season.
  • "Held" counts every attack attempt. Zone numbers are swgoh.gg's (Z2 is the fleet zone).
  • Wrong ally code saved? Click Change my ally code at the top of any report.
  • Works in desktop Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Mobile browsers usually can't run bookmarks like this.
