GAC Scout+

Scout your Grand Arena opponent in one click on swgoh.gg. Version 3.7.8.

Counter odds

Your two GAC histories blended with swgoh.gg's counters page, matched to their exact defence squad when there's enough data, and adjusted for your relic levels.

Battle plan

The best combination of your counters against the defences they usually set, never reusing a unit or anything on your defence.

Decision brief

Keep, remove or add for each of your defence squads, on all three hold-% sources, then the line-up, datacrons and a short attack plan. The short view (the default) gives one line per squad; switch to Full for everything.

Your defence

One click fills it from the defence you set in your last round; tap ✕ on anything you've changed. The plan stops using those units. Saved for rounds 2 and 3.

Their actual defence

Once their defence is up, tick the teams you can see in game and the attack plan uses exactly those instead of guessing from their history.

Defence planner

Squads you own that hold best against the attack teams your opponent uses. Keeps GLs for attack unless you say otherwise.

Zones and banners

Where they usually put each team (North, South, Trap, Fleet), and counters ranked by expected banners.

HotUtils meta

HotUtils' defence hold % for your own league (live datacrons only) loads automatically from a daily copy. It sits beside swgoh.gg Insight's division figure, and fills in where Insight is thin.

Speed check

A ⚡ warning when the team you're attacking is clearly faster than your counter (30+ speed on the fastest unit).

Datacrons

Your datacrons and theirs are in the odds: each counter and defence is rated from battles with the datacron strength each side can actually bring, and the plan never uses one datacron twice.

Export for AI

Every tab in one file, plus their round-by-round history (scores, first attack times, what held and what they dropped), ready to upload to an AI assistant.

Their attacks

What they win with and which defences stopped them.

When they attack

Their usual attack hours in your time zone, and average delay.

Roster compare

GP, GLs, relic counts, zetas, omicrons, ships, and every unit side by side, A to Z, characters and ships separately.

1. Install (once)

Computer: show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B, or Cmd+Shift+B on Mac), then drag this button onto it:

GAC Scout+

This bookmark is tiny: it loads the latest GAC Scout+ from this site each time, so you get updates without reinstalling.

Can't drag it, or on a phone? Copy the code below, bookmark any page, then edit that bookmark: name it GAC Scout+ and replace its URL with the code. If you opened this page from Discord on a phone, open it in Chrome or Safari first (Discord's built-in browser can't save bookmarks).

2. Use it

  1. Open any page on swgoh.gg. If you open your opponent's profile, their ally code fills in and it starts straight away.
  2. Click the GAC Scout+ bookmark. On Android Chrome, type GAC Scout+ in the address bar and tap the bookmark.
  3. The first time, enter your own ally code too. It's remembered in your browser, and you can change it in the header.
  4. Press Scout. The first run takes about 30 to 60 seconds. Later runs are much faster because finished rounds and swgoh.gg counters pages are cached.
  5. HotUtils' hold rates for your league load by themselves. Only if the summary says they couldn't be loaded: open the HotUtils link there, copy that page (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C) and paste it into the box.
  6. Format is Auto (their latest finished event). In the first week of a new season, set it to 3v3 or 5v5 yourself, because swgoh.gg only lists an event once it's over.
  7. Under Your defence (Battle plan tab), press Fill from my last round, then tap ✕ on anything you've changed. The battle plan won't use those units. Do this when a new GAC event starts; the list carries over to rounds 2 and 3.
  8. Once your opponent's defence is up, open Their actual defence above the attack plan and tick the teams you can see in game.

If it doesn't work

  • Nothing happens, or it says it only runs on swgoh.gg: the bookmark has to be clicked while a swgoh.gg page is open in that tab.
  • "swgoh.gg wouldn't give me...": reload the swgoh.gg page, pass its "checking your browser" step, sign in to swgoh.gg if it asks, and click the bookmark again.
  • "Couldn't find a player": check the ally code. Both players need a profile on swgoh.gg.
  • Some pages didn't load: swgoh.gg was busy. Press Scout again after a minute; pages already read are remembered.
  • No history for a new opponent: swgoh.gg may not have their GAC rounds, or they haven't played this format recently. Try the other format.

How it works

  • Battles: both players' GAC histories on swgoh.gg (up to 8 seasons, same format), plus swgoh.gg's counters page for each defence. Exact-squad data is used where it has 20+ battles, otherwise any team with that leader. Format: Auto uses their latest finished event; early in a season, pick it yourself.
  • Est. for you: swgoh.gg win rate (worth up to 30 battles) plus your two histories, ±4% per relic level vs their team (max ±12%). Data labels: solid 20+, some 6–19, thin 5 or fewer battles.
  • Battle plan: up to 18 of their likely defences (or exactly the ones you ticked under Their actual defence); every combination of your top 4 counters for the 9 most likely, then the best free counter for the rest. No unit reused; nothing on Your defence used. Most banners = win chance × banners per win.
  • Decision brief: keep/remove/add (a swap needs +3 points, an equal or better meta hold % and 20+ battles behind its score), line-up by zone, datacrons (#N) and a short attack plan. Defence as a package sends each of their attack teams at the squad it beats most easily (Hungarian method, one attack per squad). The short view gives one line per squad: the hold % that decides it and whether the three sources agree (within 5 points). Known gaps are worked out too, but only kept in the export.
  • Fill from my last round: the squads, zones and fleet from your latest round on swgoh.gg. swgoh.gg lists a round once its event is over and only records defences that were attacked, so add anything missing.
  • Suggested defence: meta squads you own at R5+ plus squads from the histories, scored half on meta hold %, half against the attack teams they use. A ranking guide, not a calibrated chance. GLs left out while the switch is on.
  • Three hold-% sources, always exact squad, leader first: swgoh.gg GAC Insight (your league and division, whole league if under 150 battles; needs a swgoh.gg login), HotUtils (whole league, live datacrons; a daily copy kept on this site, or pasted) and the swgoh.gg squads page (all leagues). ⚠ marks a better lead order. Counters never come from HotUtils.
  • Datacrons (with Insight): a datacron counts for a squad by alignment (tier 3), faction (6) or a named character (9). Each of your defence squads gets one (character matches first), never shared and kept out of attacks; the rest go to attacks, then the suggested defence. Theirs go to their likely defences, then attack teams. Expired sets are left out. Thin splits fall back to all datacrons and say so.
  • Omicrons: GAC ones only. Their defences are assumed to have all they can take; yours use only the ones you have, and battles with ones you lack are removed where there are enough.
  • Speed: the fastest unit on each side, from swgoh.gg (mods included). Flagged at 30+ speed; it doesn't change the win %.
  • Export saves every tab plus JSON, with instructions for an AI; the short version suits free tiers. Upload the file rather than pasting.
  • Can't see: mods beyond speed, turn meter luck, defences nobody attacked, and the event in progress. Everything runs in your browser.

Where the numbers come from

  • Battles from your GAC history and your opponent's (up to 8 seasons in the same format), plus swgoh.gg's GAC counters page for each of their defences.
  • Est. for you starts from the swgoh.gg win rate, adds the battles in your two histories, and is nudged up to ±12% for the relic gap between your counter and their defence. Each figure is labelled solid, some or thin data.
  • It runs on swgoh.gg itself and reads the same public pages you can open, from your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere else. HotUtils' public league meta is copied to this site once a day so the tool can read it.
  • The bookmark loads the tool's code from this site (GitHub Pages) each time you click it. The source is in the tools/gac-scout folder of the repository.
  • With swgoh.gg GAC Insight (sign in to swgoh.gg), datacrons are in the win %: both players' datacrons are read, and each number comes from this season's battles where each side had a datacron of the strength they can actually bring to that team. GAC omicrons count too: your opponent's defences are assumed to have every GAC omicron (worst case), and your defences use the ones you have. Mods aren't in the odds (only a speed warning), so treat them as a guide. For what to build on your own account, see AhnaldT101's datacron guide.
