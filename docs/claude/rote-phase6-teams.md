Sources: Copilot and Perplexity searches. Every "verified" lineup was checked by Claude on the squad-selection screen in the video (frame at the timestamp given), or is Sean's own tested team. Not yet added to the map (except Hoth Aphra).

## Requirements
The fan-maintained requirement list (github.com/brent-law/swgoh-ROTE-calculator, `ROTE_manual_mission_confirmations_2026-05-01.md`) **matches the map's planet data exactly** for all 15 missions.
- **Hoth Aphra (hoth:c4) is a Combat Mission**: verified on the in-game panel in Songeta's 6 Apr 2026 showcase at 7:05.
- Scarif Cassian (c2) and Baze (c6): one video calls them special missions; the requirement file and Nov 2026 guides say combat; the map says combat. Not checked in game.
- Death Star fleet: Imperial TIE Fighter must be one of the starting three (the checker only checks you own it).
