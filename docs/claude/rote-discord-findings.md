# ROTE: what the "Slice of Otoh Gunga" Discord TB Library has that the map doesn't (read 20 Nov 2026)

Read-only sweep of the Territory Battles Library: 7 index channels (phase-1…6, bonus-planets), 76 mission threads (mostly Oct 2024 – Aug 2025, curated by Egnards), tb-help-corner and tb-submissions-channel (latest ~14 messages each, Mar–Nov 2026). Text only, not screenshots or videos. Death Star and Scarif have no threads; Hoth Jabba/Aphra, Kafrene Fleet/Cassian-K2/Imperial Officer, Mandalore DTMG threads are empty.
Compared with js/guide_data.js (swgohrote.com Nov 2026). Sean: keep for the next ROTE and use as needed, not built into the map yet.

## Game rules checked (verified 20 Nov 2026)
- **A unit is used once per phase across all planets** (combat, special or operation) and deploys to one territory (swgoh.wiki Territory Battle page; EA forum).
- **Operations (platoons)**: a platoon unit counts as that unit's one use, so it can't fight anywhere that phase. The planner only warns; an optional "donated units" tick list was declined as extra work for Sean (revisit if wanted).
- **Special missions: one attempt per TB per player, win or lose** (EA forum, Nov 2026; a crash also uses the attempt). Planner implication: let the player mark specials already attempted this TB.
- **Unstarred planets stay open; paths progress independently** (swgoh.wiki). Matches the "Planets open now" ticks.
- **Ground missions need 5 units** (Sean confirmed in game). Exceptions: Lord Vader solo, Darth Vader solo, Bracca Cere + Cal, Tatooine Mandalore unlock (3).
- Ships vs pilots don't compete: no source either way; the planner keeps them separate.

## Notes and warnings worth showing on the map
- Coruscant Jedi CM: don't use JML's ultimate in wave 1; a final blow on Clone Commander with it can soft-lock the game.
- Zeffo open: Boss Nass needs Tarpals' omicron to win on auto. JMK needs Padmé.
- Tatooine open: Cere team needs JKCK's cleanse.
- Tatooine Reva SM: an extra Pirate spawns if the planet's platoons aren't done. GI + Marrok (omicron) 95% manual.
- Dathomir Merrin SM: Old Daka lead almost mandatory; high-health mods, R8 helps.
- Bracca Jedi CM (JML): 90% with 6/6 Coruscant platoons, 25% with 0/6.
- Mustafar fleet: Leviathan only ~60% on auto; Chimaera 95% manual.
- Kessel fleet: Executor with Ghost; open with Executor's heal to put taunt on Hound's Tooth, then Xanadu Blood's breach makes it stick (95% manual).
- Tatooine Jabba CM: Jabba can solo it at R7 on auto.
- Felucia Jabba CM: IG-90's dodges let random Hutt Cartel / Bounty Hunters win.
- Platoon effects: Mustafar's bombardment hits Mustafar and Geonosis; Coruscant's "The Path" boosts Jedi on Coruscant and Bracca; Corellia's shipyards weaken enemy ships in all mixed-path fleet missions; Mandalore's 4/6+ mostly help Mandalorians only.
